# v0.15.0 (unreleased)

## Delegated runs

- **ES256 delegation statements** (spec RFC 0030's amendment, §2, *Signing with a P-256 key*). A
  delegation link may be signed with a P-256 key and hold `alg: "ES256"`; it is verified at low S,
  and a chain may mix Ed25519 and ES256 links. Ed25519 links verify exactly as before.
- **Pinned keys carry their algorithm.** `ContextInput.principal.keys` accepts the gateway's pin
  form for a P-256 key, `{"alg": "ES256", "key": <66 hex>}`, beside the unchanged 64-hex Ed25519
  form. The algorithm comes from the pin, never from a key's length.
- **A chain bound to one scope identifier** (#373). A root may hold `scope.scopeId`, which every
  link repeats. Under a bound chain every gateway call is sent with that scope identifier
  (`gatewayExecutionContext(signal).scope`); a gateway source mapped to another scope identifier,
  or to none (`gatewayScope.scope`), is refused `scope-id-mismatch` before it leaves the harness;
  and a verified receipt of another scope is withheld. A gateway source whose receipts the package
  does not verify (no pinned `gatewayReceipt`; a host `verifyReceipt` alone) is refused when the
  bound principal is set, with a `delegation refused` guardrail (`scope-id-mismatch`), and none of
  its calls is sent.
- **Credentials.** The gateway credential is sent by the host's executor, not the library. The
  library's own credential seam is `bindModelCall`, which reads `sessionBearer()` for each request; a
  test holds a credential rotated between two requests of one session. The refusal name is added to
  `run-principal.json#/$defs/refusal`.
- The specification's frozen statement cases (55 ES256, 39 Ed25519) are copied under
  `contract/testdata/principal/` with their citation, and the package's own scope-binding vectors
  are in `contract/vectors/principal/scope-binding.json`.
